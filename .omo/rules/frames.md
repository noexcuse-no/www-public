# Frame Pages

Full spec: `.specs/frames/README.md`

Frame pages are `_pages/*.md` with `class: frame` + `layout: article`. Key: `frame_id` (matches permalink slug), `title`, `description`, `hero` (image/alt/title/intro), `json_ld` (Article list, author "No Excuse AS"), `color_accent`, `weight`, `questions`. No `_frames/` collection, no perspektiv layout.
